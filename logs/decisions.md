## Phase 1 nearest-neighbor sanity note (2026-10-16)

Inspected top-5 neighbors (from the N=100K exact ground truth) for 3 test queries at D=1536 and D=128:

- **q0** "what did the russian new jersey nets owner tell jason kidd when he fired him": at D=1536 all 5 neighbors are Jason-Kidd / NBA passages (topically correct). At **D=128 the neighbors collapse to unrelated passages** (Kevin O'Leary, iOffer, a free-agent dictionary entry) — the 128-prefix loses the fine-grained relational signal for this harder query.
- **q100** "activation price stop limit order": both D=1536 and D=128 return stop/limit-order finance passages; only the ranking order differs slightly.
- **q500** "calcium and carbon from what molecule": both dimensions return calcium-carbonate / calcium chemistry passages; minor reordering at D=128.

Conclusion: embeddings are topically sensible at D=1536 (sanity passes). Matryoshka truncation to D=128 preserves easy topical queries but degrades harder relational queries — exactly the recall-vs-dimension effect the study measures. Phase 1 neighbor-sanity acceptance met.
